## Changes from the first model

- Replaced plain chair blocks with rounded upholstered seats/backs, metal arms,
  individual legs and lower frame members.
- Added an opening for the observed side entrance near the stage, open door
  leaves, entrance steps, jambs and railings. Its exact position is estimated.
- Added green/gold stage fascia, tables, ceremonial chairs, display frames,
  flowers, stage-light battens and spotlight bodies.
- Refined beige pleated curtains, small square acoustic tiles, ceiling joints,
  dark ventilation grilles and the white suspended ceiling.
- Added porch arch lobes, window frames/niches, barred gates, garden paving,
  hedge rows and benches.
- Added procedural wood/paving/grass surfaces and rendered shadows in the
  interactive viewer; added day/evening lighting and entrance/garden views.

The existing satellite-based building positions were retained. Furniture counts,
building dimensions/heights, bay counts and interior orientation remain
estimated. Construction-like details do not turn this into a surveyed replica.
The videos do not locate every toilet, kitchen, service room or guest room.
