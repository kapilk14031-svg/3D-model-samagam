The auditorium plaque spelling **Chandermani** follows the visible source,
even though some public reports use a different transliteration. Portraits and
unreadable/occluded words have not been fabricated on the recreated boards.

## Entry and inner circulation

Open gate leaves now have individual bars, raised finials, ornamental rings,
hinges and rails. Added the entrance apron, gate-side bay and beam downlights.
The internal concrete approach, hall-side pedestrian paving, garden-edge
circulation, residential turning court, curb joints and illustrative drain
grilles are separate geometry. Drain positions and construction details are
visual approximations, not an engineering road or drainage survey.

The gate-to-portico sequence also improves the alignment of the hall side
entrance toward the residential end. Exact registration still needs a plan.

## Surrounding area

Added visually read massing of neighboring industrial roofs and roof seams,
agricultural parcels, rural access tracks, the landscaped enclosure north of
the road, and its irregular path pattern. Google Maps' standard map view was
used to confirm that the eastern diagonal corridor is a road; no railway has
been added there. Building heights, parcel boundaries and ownership remain
unverified.

Birmi Industries and Birmi Impex are shown as **map listing labels**, not as
invented painted names on their roofs or facades. The locality is also supported
by the company's [official contact page](https://birmi.in/contact-us/).
