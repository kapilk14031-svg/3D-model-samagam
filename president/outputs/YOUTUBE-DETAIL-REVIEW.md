## Changes in this revision

The main hall now has diffuser louvres/rims, recessed-light bezels, speaker mounting/grille details, entrance pulls and separate ground-level front sofas. Seat/acoustic fabric and warmer wood finish are browser rendering refinements; baked procedural material textures are also embedded in the GLB. The existing stage furniture, green steps/fascia, square acoustic panels, curtains, roof grid and chair frames were retained after comparison.

Added a partial **Tapasya Dham — andar** view with curtains, circular floor, meditation sheet, furniture, display frame, vases and hanging ornament. The blank display area intentionally does not pretend to reproduce the portrait/artwork.

Compound improvements include green low bollards and brown decorative lanterns with white hanging bowls. The small red-roof structure gained pitched roof, veranda posts, green frontage and steps. Its correspondence to the video hut is a visual interpretation and needs site confirmation. Gate size and satellite-registered core footprints remain retained.
