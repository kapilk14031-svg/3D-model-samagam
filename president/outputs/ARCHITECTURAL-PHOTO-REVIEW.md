## Remaining limits

Source coverage is much better, but the reconstruction is not a survey or an exact photogrammetric replica. Metric dimensions, petal/step/window counts and detailed placement are estimated. The full finial emblem, tiny painted words, exact curved ornament, rear faces and interior of the lotus pavilion are not completely resolved. No guarantee that every hidden feature has been recorded is supported by these images. The historical health-centre facade is a separate study; assigning it to a guessed campus roof would create false location information.
