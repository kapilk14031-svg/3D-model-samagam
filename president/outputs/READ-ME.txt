WHAT IS ESTIMATED / NOT YET VERIFIED
This is a schematic planning model, not a measured digital twin, LiDAR model,
photogrammetric reconstruction or an exact extraction of Google 3D geometry.
The current footprint frame uses the supplied displayed graticule; no certified
ground survey was supplied. See the new GeoJSON for digitised footprint extents.
Local coordinates use metres with +X east, +Y up and +Z south.
Heights, facade bay count, detailed facade placement/orientation
of the interior within its roof footprint and 840 illustrative chairs are estimates.
The chair count is NOT a verified or approved audience capacity.
Nearby structures are context; ownership/site inclusion and function unverified.
The compound wall is a visual envelope, not a legal property boundary.
Toilets, existing kitchen/dining, utility rooms, guest-room inventory and all
remaining interiors could not be located from the supplied imagery.
Do not treat this model as proof that every room or facility is accounted for.
