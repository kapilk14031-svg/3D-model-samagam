# Proposed helipad and vehicle approach

Added 21 October 2026 as a user-requested visual concept.

- Centre is 100 m from the registered main gate, on the opposite side of Assan-Thirana Road in the field context.
- Area matches the modeled central garden/lawn footprint: approximately 51.9 x 47.3 m (2,454 square metres). These are screenshot-derived model dimensions.
- A static white/blue helicopter has cabin glazing, side windows, handles, landing skids, tail boom/fin, main rotor and tail rotor.
- A proposed 5.5 m vehicle-access strip links the helipad-area edge to the main gate.
- Red carpet has two separate routes: helicopter side to the edge of the helipad access road, and main gate to auditorium entrance, including stair treads. The vehicle road between field and gate has no red carpet.
- Choose **Helipad, helicopter & vehicle access - proposed** in Camera Perspectives to see the layout.

The location, aircraft and access are illustrative planning geometry, not an existing helipad or verified flight/land suitability. Existing police deployment in the SQLite backend is unchanged.

Coordinates, footprint and route points are saved in helipad-proposal.json; proposed geometry is also included in the updated venue GLB.
